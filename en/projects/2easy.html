2easy Portugal

Real Estate Network

In my collaboration with 2easy Portugal, I was responsible for adapting and expanding the brand's visual communication after the new identity was implemented. My services included updating and creating a wide variety of materials, keeping visual consistency across all brand touchpoints.

I did the layout of magazines, data sheets, presentations, flyers, institutional documents and large-format materials such as banners, billboards, tarpaulins, vehicle decals and shop windows.

I also developed templates and creatives for social media, plus covers and profile images for both 2easy Portugal and its agencies.

All the work was designed to ensure a solid, cohesive and visually strategic brand presence.
