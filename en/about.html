About me

Hi, I'm Tay.

Tayane Morgana, or simply Tay, graphic and digital designer specialized in Visual Identity, Social Media Design and UI/UX Design, with around 10 years of experience.

Throughout my career, I have had the privilege of working with many companies in Brazil, in a wide range of niches, always adapting my creative vision to each client's needs.

Recently, I led a multidisciplinary team in the Information Technology area at Petrobras, where I not only delivered impactful results but also developed creative and innovative strategies.

With a personalized and human-centered approach, I stand out for turning ideas into visual solutions that connect brands and people. My unique vision and design expertise make me an essential partner for businesses looking for creativity, accessibility and innovation.

I believe design goes beyond aesthetics; it is a way to create memorable experiences, communicate values and turn dreams into reality.
